How to play

  • Aim: put your finger anywhere and drag down. The bar bends like a slingshot and dots show the shot.
  • Fire: let go. Pull further for a faster shot; the path is the same either way.
  • Break bricks: each hit takes one off a brick's number. At zero it breaks.
  • Collect balls: touch a floating ball to add one to your shots from the next level.
  • Hold the line: after each shot the bricks move down a row. When one reaches the bar, the game is over.

Common questions

Will I lose my game if I leave the app?
No. It's saved when you leave and after every level, and it carries on exactly where you were.

How do I start a new game?
Tap ↻ in the top right, then tap it again to confirm.

Where are my records kept?
On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them.
