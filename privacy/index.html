Defender

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Defender doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.

What the app keeps

Your best level, your most bricks broken in one level, and the game in progress are saved only on your iPhone, so you can pick up where you left off. They never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No internet connection: the game works entirely offline.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
