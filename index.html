Defender

Aim, bounce, hold the line.

Pull back, aim, and let go. Your ball bounces off the walls and smashes into numbered bricks. Each hit knocks a number down by one, and at zero the brick breaks.

After every shot the bricks move one row closer. Collect the floating balls to add to your stream, line up the angles, and see how many levels you can hold them off.

  • One-finger slingshot controls: the bar bends as you pull.
  • Every brick's colour shows how tough it is.
  • Leave any time. Your game is right where you left it.
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking.
